Your ad, shown while AI is thinking.

WaitMint is a browser extension that shows one clean, clearly-labelled sponsored card during the few seconds an AI chat tool is generating its answer — then it disappears the moment the reply starts. No banners. No popups. No interruption.

How it works

Three seconds of honest attention, instead of another ignored banner.

1

You ask, AI thinks

You send a prompt on your favourite AI chat site. While the model generates — usually a few seconds — the answer area is empty and you are simply waiting.

2

One card appears

WaitMint shows a single sponsored card, clearly tagged “Ad”, near the answer area. It never covers your composer, never moves, and has an obvious close button.

3

Answer arrives, card leaves

The instant the response starts streaming, the card fades away on its own. At most one card per prompt. That is the whole product.

The privacy promise

WaitMint never reads your prompts or the AI’s answers. It only detects whether the page is in a “generating” state — the same thing you can see with your own eyes. Your stats (cards shown) stay on your device. Advertisers receive only aggregate impression and click counts for their own ads: no browsing history, no identifiers, nothing personal. Every control — global on/off, per-site toggles — is one click away in the popup.
