How it works

Deliberately old-fashioned: a person reviews your ad before it runs.

1

Submit the form

Tell us your ad copy, link and budget. Submitting is free and takes two minutes. No payment is taken now — there is no checkout on this page.

2

We review it manually

A human checks the creative and the landing page. If it’s a fit, we email you an invoice with a payment link. If it isn’t, we tell you why — no silent rejections.

3

Pay the invoice, go live

Once the invoice is paid, your card enters the live WaitMint feed across your chosen platforms and runs until your budget is spent. You get impression/click reports as it runs.

Also accepted: network & direct deals

Run an ad network, an agency, or want a direct placement deal? We also onboard creatives through managed placements: the network or advertiser supplies finished creatives (headline, copy, link, optional image), we review them like any other campaign, and they run in the same feed under your network’s name. Onboarding is manual today — write to contact@visionquantech.com and we’ll set it up with you. To be clear: no ad network is pre-integrated or auto-serving on WaitMint — every network creative is registered and reviewed by us first, because the extension only ever displays data, never third-party ad scripts.
