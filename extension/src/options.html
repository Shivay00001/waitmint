Custom AI sites (generic detector)

Enable WaitMint on any other AI chat site. The generic detector looks for stop buttons, streaming indicators and busy regions — the same loading-state signals used on supported sites.

    How the money works

    Advertiser revenue on WaitMint is split three ways:

    • 50% — platform: WaitMint / VisionQuantech, for infrastructure and operations.
    • 40% — users: shared with the people whose browsers show the cards — that's you.
    • 10% — cited sources: the websites and creators whose content the AI answers draw on.

    At a $12 CPM, 1,000 impressions earn $12.00 gross: $6.00 platform, $4.80 to the users pool, $1.20 to the cited-sources pool. Your accrual is $0.0048 per impression, credited against your anonymous install ID, so the $10 payout threshold takes roughly 2,084 impressions. House promos earn nothing. Payouts in v1 are manual and start at a $10 balance. The popup shows your real backend balance — if you're offline it shows local impression counts instead of guessing a number.

    Privacy — full privacy, by design

    WaitMint never reads, stores or transmits your prompts, the AI's responses, page content, or your browsing history. Detection uses loading-state signals only (stop buttons, streaming indicators, busy regions) — the extension does not inspect what you type or what the AI answers.

    The only data that ever leaves your browser is an ad event: ad ID + site host (e.g. "chatgpt.com") + your anonymous install ID. No full URLs, no conversation IDs, no text. Impression and click counts are also stored locally in your browser so the popup can show them offline. Full policy: see PRIVACY.md shipped with the extension.
