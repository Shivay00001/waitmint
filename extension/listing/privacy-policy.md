# WaitMint — Privacy Policy (store listing copy)

_Last updated: 2026-10-20 · Publisher: VisionQuantech (https://visionquantech.com)_

## Full privacy, by design

WaitMint never reads, stores or transmits your prompts, the AI's responses,
page content, or your browsing history. The extension detects one thing only:
whether an AI chat page is in a "thinking / generating" loading state, using
interface signals (stop buttons, streaming indicators, busy regions). It does
not inspect what you type or what the AI answers.

## Data transmitted

When a sponsored card is shown or clicked, one ad event is sent to the
WaitMint backend (visionquantech.com) containing only:

- an **anonymous install ID** (a random UUID generated on first run, not
  linked to your identity) — used to credit your 40% user share of revenue;
- the **ad ID** of the creative shown/clicked;
- the **site hostname** (e.g. `chatgpt.com`) — never a full URL, path, or
  conversation ID;
- the event type (`impression` or `click`).

No analytics SDKs, no fingerprinting, no third-party trackers, and no remote
JavaScript — ad creatives are delivered as data and rendered by the
extension's own bundled code.

## Data stored locally

Settings, per-day/total impression and click counters, your anonymous
install ID, and a cached ad feed — all in your browser's extension storage,
deleted on uninstall.

## Earnings

Advertiser revenue split: 50% platform / 40% users / 10% cited sources.
Balances are computed server-side from the anonymous events above; manual
payouts from $10 in v1.

## Chrome Web Store data-use disclosures

- Does not collect or use: personally identifiable information, health
  information, financial/payment information, authentication information,
  **personal communications (prompts/responses — never accessed)**,
  location, web history, or page content.
- Collected (anonymous, for the item's single purpose): activity —
  impression/click counts tied to an anonymous install ID.
- Data is not sold to third parties and is not used for purposes unrelated
  to the item's core functionality.
